# Classroom poll images

The PNG files are high-resolution exports of the editable SVG files with the same names.

- `squashing-functions`: compare possible mappings from a real-valued score to a bounded output. All three are bounded; monotonicity and training behavior distinguish them. Bounded outputs alone do not establish calibration.
- `loss-function-options`: compare training objectives. Cross-entropy, Brier score, and hinge loss can be used for classification, with different interpretations. Minimizing signed error alone encourages larger predictions regardless of the observed label.
- `ood-imagenet-fooling`: a teaching adaptation of Figure 1 from Nguyen, Yosinski, and Clune, [*Deep Neural Networks Are Easily Fooled: High Confidence Predictions for Unrecognizable Images*](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2015/html/Nguyen_Deep_Neural_Networks_2015_CVPR_paper.html), CVPR 2015. The embedded research image is credited to its original authors.
- `ood-two-moons-author-figure`: a teaching adaptation of the confidence illustration from Hein, Andriushchenko, and Bitterwolf, [*Why ReLU Networks Yield High-Confidence Predictions Far Away from the Training Data and How to Mitigate the Problem*](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Hein_Why_ReLU_Networks_Yield_High-Confidence_Predictions_Far_Away_From_the_CVPR_2019_paper.html), CVPR 2019. The embedded research image is credited to its original authors.

The research figures illustrate the cited authors' results; they are not outputs of the mushroom notebook. Third-party figures retain their original rights and attribution.
